About Saltwood Construction

Local. Veteran-Owned. Built Right.

Saltwood Construction is a veteran and locally owned residential and commercial construction company based in Brunswick County, North Carolina.

We specialize in custom homes, renovations, kitchen and bathroom remodels, and general contracting. Every project is built with attention to detail, clear communication, and a commitment to quality that stands the test of time.

Unlike cookie-cutter builders, we work with you to design and build a home that fits your life, your land, and your vision.

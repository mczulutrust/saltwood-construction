About Saltwood Construction

Veteran and locally owned residential and commercial construction company based in Brunswick County, North Carolina.

We specialize in new home construction, renovations, kitchen and bathroom remodels, custom builds, and general contracting. Serving Brunswick County and surrounding areas with quality craftsmanship and reliable service.
